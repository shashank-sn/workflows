# Checklist

## Before writing

- [ ] The title is a question the viewer would type, 100 characters or fewer.
- [ ] The viewer is named by role and knows only the title and the company.
- [ ] The one idea is something the viewer has not thought of.
- [ ] Every fact has a source. The playlist plan exists and is approved.

## The script

- [ ] Opens with the title question and the company once, as publisher.
- [ ] Parts are named in about thirty words, five at most.
- [ ] No chapter is numbered aloud. Each starts from where the story is.
- [ ] Each chapter's question is read aloud and answered in a full sentence.
- [ ] Each trade term is explained once, then used the same way.
- [ ] At most three numbers, each with what it measured.
- [ ] The close gives the answer, the article line and the next title.

## The checks

- [ ] Flow read, fact check, missing-context read, cut, second fact check, each by a fresh reader.
- [ ] Every flagged line is fixed or kept with a reason.
- [ ] The rough cut voices the current script, last row included.

## Packaging and handover

- [ ] Description, chapters (close included), 10 to 16 tags, thumbnail, end screen.
- [ ] Hard words and the company's name have a confirmed pronunciation.
- [ ] The builder's copy leaves out the question chain and fact notes.
- [ ] The approver said the rough cut is good before the handover went out.
