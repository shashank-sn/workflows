# Playlist standard

Every video in a playlist follows one standard, so each one closes into the next and the builder never has to guess.

## The stages

Each stage ends at a gate. Nothing moves on until the gate passes.

| Stage | What is made | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Set up | Newest method loaded, past corrections read, voice and the company's name checked | the package check for setup passes |
| 2. The playlists | Every playlist the company could have, ranked, plus playlist 1's plan | the approver picks which goes first |
| 3. Video 1 | The look, the script through every check, the packaging, a rough cut | the package check for review passes |
| 4. Review page | One page: listen first, then look, script, how it goes up, the playlist | the approver says it is good |
| 5. Builder's page | The page and handover, made from approved files | the package check for builder passes, then the approver sends it |
| 6. The build | The builder's video, checked against the handover | every line and chapter time matches |
| 7. Client sample | The playlist and the built video 1 | the approver decides what goes out, and when |

## The minimum plan

For one video with no plan, write every title in watch order, with the one idea and the next title for each. Stage 2 and the review page are for a full playlist.

## The plan

Each video in the plan has the same fields: tracked question and source article, who it is for, the one idea, the story, chapter questions, the fair alternative, the company's part, the hero picture, and the next video.

Titles are the question a buyer would type, trimmed to 100 characters. Choose playlists where video already shows up for the buyer's questions. Open each playlist on the result the buyer wants, not on a definition, unless the subject is new to them.

Write the playlist's look once, in one world file, and paste it into every page and handover when they are built. Never keep a second copy.

## The review page

Sections in order: listen first, the look, video 1's script with a comment box per chapter, how it goes up on the platform, the playlist with one card per video. One version number and one word count, read from the handover and never typed.

## The handover

1. What this is
2. What we would like from you
3. What carries over to every video
4. How to say the hard words
5. The picture
6. Packaging
7. The script
8. For us: the question chain and story beats
9. For us: what can and cannot be said
10. For us: what the checks found
11. For us: history

The builder drops sections 8 to 11. The script table is the only copy of the script.

## The close, every video

The answer to the title, then word for word: "If you'd like to learn more, we've linked to an article with more details in the description." Then "Next in this playlist: <next title>". The last video leaves out the next title. Cut the article line at upload only if the article is not live.

## Keep one copy

Write what differs by company (its line, never-say list, length range, voice, look) in one folder per company. Keep method in one shared place. A fix made in one copy of the method reaches the others only by hand, so keep one copy.
