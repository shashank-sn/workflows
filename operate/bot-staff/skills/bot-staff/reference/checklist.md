# Checklist

## Audit

- [ ] Every routine is listed with schedule, what it touches and what it reports.
- [ ] Counts are from logs or marked "stated by owner".
- [ ] Each routine is marked keep, merge or drop with a reason.

## Job sheets

- [ ] One job per bot, with one owner for each output.
- [ ] "Never does" is filled in for every bot.
- [ ] Connections are the smallest set the job needs.
- [ ] Each bot has its own memory file or notes.

## Outside the company

- [ ] Every outward action is listed and needs the owner's yes.
- [ ] Never-invent and never-delete rules are in every job sheet.
- [ ] Prices and other held facts are named as never going outside.

## Staff

- [ ] One shared record is named, and each bot writes to it.
- [ ] Handoffs go bot to bot.
- [ ] The chief of staff routes and does not do other bots' jobs.
- [ ] The stand-up has done, blocked, needs and a cleanup pass.

## Gates (if the staff builds)

- [ ] Scope cut is dated and approved before the build.
- [ ] Skeptic has at most 5 objections, each with evidence, and at most 2 rounds.
- [ ] QA tests the done-when checks before reading code.
- [ ] The merge card gives merge, hand to a human reviewer or hold.

## Record

- [ ] Corrections keep the old text with a dated note.
